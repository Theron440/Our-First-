CHAPTER VIII

Personal Development and Relationship Development 🩵🩹⚕️🖤

One thing I’m learning about love is that being close doesn’t mean we have to become the same person. We each have our own thoughts, interests, goals, and ways of seeing the world, and I love that there’s still so much about you and me to discover.

Gusto kong suportahan ka habang tinutupad mo ang mga bagay na mahalaga sa’yo, kahit may mga pagkakataong kailangan mong maglaan ng oras at lakas para sa sarili mong mga pangarap. I want you to know that I will always support you, and I hope you’ll always feel free to be yourself with me—not only the happy parts, but also the uncertain, changing, still-figuring-it-out parts.

I don’t want us to hold each other back from growing. I want us to make room for that growth, to celebrate the person you’re becoming, and to keep sharing who we are along the way. We can have our own paths and still meet each other with love, curiosity, and respect.

My love for you isn’t about asking you to stay exactly the same. It’s about wanting to know you through every version of yourself—the person you are now and the person you’re still becoming. And I hope we can keep growing not because we have to, but because we feel safe enough to become more fully ourselves.

I’m proud of the person you are. You’re such a great person, really, and I’m excited to see you grow and achieve all of your dreams. May sarili tayong mga pangarap at landas, pero masaya akong magkasabay tayong lumalago—bilang ikaw, bilang ako, at bilang tayo.
