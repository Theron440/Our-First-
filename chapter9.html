CHAPTER IX

The Distance Between Two Hearts 🩵 📏 🖤

Sometimes I wish I could be beside you, not just through a screen, but close enough to share the same place, the same little moments, and the same day. I miss you so much, especially when I think about all the things we could do together if we had more time and the chance to meet again.

May mga araw na gusto ko na lang na magkasama tayo, kahit simpleng tambay o kuwentuhan lang. I look forward to the vacations, free time, and opportunities when we might finally see each other again and make memories side by side. Even imagining those moments gives me something lovely to look forward to.

Still, distance hasn’t erased the closeness we’ve built. Our messages, calls, little updates, conversations, and games help us share parts of our everyday lives, even when we’re apart. I treasure the way we keep finding small ways to be present for each other.

I’ve learned that closeness isn’t only about being in the same place. It’s also about remembering each other, making time when we can, asking how the other person is doing, and carrying that connection through ordinary days. Kahit may distansya sa pagitan natin, may mga paraan pa rin para maramdaman kong malapit ka sa akin.

I miss you, and I look forward to seeing you again. Until then, I’ll keep treasuring the moments we can share and holding close the hope of all the moments still waiting for us.
