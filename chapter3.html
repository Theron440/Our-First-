CHAPTER III

Ang Mga Mumunting Sandali

When I think about us, I don’t only remember the “big milestones.” Iniisip ko rin yung mga little moments too—the conversations that made me smile, the times we made each other laugh, and the simple ways we found to be part of each other’s days.

Some moments may have seemed ordinary when they happened, but they became enchanted because I shared them with you. A message from you could brighten my day. A conversation could make time pass really quickly. Even when we were just talking about little things, I felt happy knowing I got to share that moment with you.

Sa bawat usap, tawanan, at bonding natin, unti-unti tayong nakagawa ng mga alaalang “atin lang.” Those moments helped make our relationship feel more real and more us. Sila yung mga nagpapaalala sa akin na love isn’t only found in grand gestures—it can also be found in the small ways we listen, care, and make time for each other.

I’m thankful for every memory we’ve made, whether it was a big moment or just a quiet part of an ordinary day. Each one has become a little piece of our story, something I can look back on and keep close to my heart.

I hope we keep making more memories together—more moments that make us laugh, more conversations that bring us closer, and more ordinary days that feel special simply because we have each other.

When I look back on this first year, I know I’ll remember not just what we did, but how it felt to share those moments with you. And among all the little things that made this year special, one of my favorites will always be that they were moments with you.
