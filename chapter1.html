CHAPTER I

The Beginning

✦

Every love story has a beginning, and this is ours.

Before we became “us,” we were two strangers whose paths somehow crossed and all of that was thankfully to Litmatch. I may not have known then how important you would become to me, but little by little, you started to feel like someone I wanted to keep close, not just in my thoughts, but in my life.

Somewhere along the way, talking to you became something I looked forward to. Your presence became familiar and comforting, and the thought of sharing little parts of my day with you started to mean more than I could explain. It wasn’t one single moment that made you special to me. It was everything, slowly adding up to this , the conversations, the laughter, the care, and the way my feelings for you kept growing.

That’s one of the things I love about our beginning. It brought us here, to a love that is ours. We learned about each other, discovered what makes us happy, and found our own way of being together. We became more than two people sharing moments; we became partners, best friends, and a part of each other’s everyday lives.

When I look back at the beginning of our story, I feel really grateful. Grateful for every step that brought us closer, every chance we had to understand each other better, and every moment that helped our relationship grow. Even the small things became part of something meaningful because I got to share them with you.

I’m glad it was you, my love . I’m glad our story began, and I’m glad that through all the days that have passed, we have continued writing it together. Our beginning may be only the first part of our story, but it will always be special to me because it led me to you.

This is where our story began—and I’m so glad it did.🖤
