CHAPTER VII

Tough Love 🩵👊🏻🖤

Not every season of love feels full of long conversations and exciting stories. Minsan saglit lang, walang gana, at hindi ganun ka-saya; the reason could be dahil pagod lang tayo. May mga araw na walang time para mag-usap, may mga reply na simple, at may mga pagkakataong parang wala tayong bagong iku-kuwento. I want to be honest: those dry moments can sometimes make me miss the way we used to talk.

But I’m learning that a quieter season doesn’t mean our love has become less real. Sometimes it simply means life is asking more from us—our time, our energy, our attention. Even then, I can still feel your care in the small things: a quick check-in, a little update, or the way we find our way back to each other when the day finally slows down.

I don’t expect us to be available every moment, to be full of energy, or to always have the perfect words. I want us to give each other room to be tired and busy without assuming the worst. Kung minsan maikli lang ang usapan natin, sana maalala nating hindi nasusukat sa haba ng messages ang halaga ng pagmamahal natin. We must be patient while still making small efforts to stay connected.

My promise is to meet these slower days with gentleness. I’ll try to ask how you’re really doing, listen without rushing you, and understand when you need time to rest. And when I’m the one feeling worn out, I hope I can let you know instead of pulling away without explanation. We don’t have to fix every quiet or those dry moments; we can simply be honest and kind with each other through it. Let's be gentle to each other, please.

Kahit may mga araw na tahimik ang mundo natin, gusto kong patuloy tayong magtagpo roon—in a simple kumusta, sa kaunting pag-unawa, at sa pagpiling bumalik sa isa’t isa kapag kaya na natin. I want to keep learning how to love you not only when everything feels easy, but also when life gets busy and all we can offer is a little patience and care.

Because even in the silent and darkest phase of our life and relationship, you still matter to me. And I want us to keep making space for each other, gently and honestly, through every season.
