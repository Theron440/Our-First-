CHAPTER VI

Ikaw at Ako, Sa Bawat Yugto 🩵🍀🖤

Looking back on this first year, I feel so grateful for everything we’ve shared: the laughter, the conversations, the little surprises, the games we played, and all the moments that brought us closer. Each one has become part of a story I’ll always hold close to my heart.

Thank you for being you, my lunch Luther. Thank you for letting me know you, for sharing your time and heart with me, and for making even the simplest days feel special. I hope you know how much I appreciate you and how happy I am that we get to share this journey together.

Hindi man laging perpekto ang lahat, ang mahalaga sa akin ay patuloy nating pinipili ang isa’t isa. I want to keep learning, growing, and making memories with you—sa mga masasayang araw at maging sa mga panahong kailangan lang natin ng kaunting pag-unawa at lambing.

Happy first anniversary, my love. This year has given me so many beautiful memories, and I’m excited for all the ones we have yet to make. Wherever our story takes us, I hope we keep meeting each new chapter hand in hand.

Ikaw pa rin ang pipiliin ko, ngayon, bukas, at sa bawat kabanata ng ating kuwento.
