CHAPTER II

Getting to Know You

As we spent more time talking, I started discovering more and more about you. Every conversation gave me another little piece of who you are—your thoughts, your feelings, the things that make you happy, and the little details that make you “you.”

I loved getting to know you little by little. There was always something new to learn, and even the simplest conversations could make me feel closer to you. I started to look forward to hearing from you and sharing parts of my day with you. Somehow, talking to you became one of those little things that could make an ordinary day feel special.

The more I learned about you, the more I appreciated having you in my life. I began to notice the way you made me feel cared for, how comfortable it felt to talk to you, and how naturally you became someone I wanted to share things with. It felt good to have someone who could make me laugh, listen to me, and be there through the little moments of everyday life.

Getting to know each other also meant learning how we think, what we feel, and how we each show love. We didn’t have to know everything about one another all at once. We just kept sharing, listening, and learning as we went. Kada usap natin, bonding, and every moment, mas naging meaningful yung connection natin—and umaasa ako na lalo pa itong lalalim.

I’m grateful for all the little things that helped me know you better. They may have seemed ordinary at the time, but together they became part of what made our relationship special and deeper. Each one brought us a little closer and gave me another reason to care about you even more.

And even after this first year, I still want to keep discovering more about you. There will always be something new to learn, another story to hear, and another side of you to love. Getting to know you wasn’t just part of how our story began—it’s something I hope we keep doing for all the chapters ahead.
