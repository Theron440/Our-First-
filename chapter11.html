CHAPTER XI

If Life Ever Becomes Heavy Again ❤️‍🩹

I know life won’t always feel easy. There may be days when we’re tired, busy, worried, or unsure of what comes next. There may be times when we need patience, understanding, and a little more care from each other.

If life ever feels heavy again, I hope we remember that we don’t have to face everything alone. We can talk honestly, listen without rushing, and remind each other that we’re on the same team and that partner tayo lagi. We don’t have to solve everything all at once. Sometimes, being there for each other and taking things one day at a time is enough.

Gusto kong suportahan ka habang hinaharap mo ang mga pagsubok at tinutupad ang mga pangarap mo. I hope you’ll support me in the same way as I continue growing, too. We can keep becoming our own people while still encouraging each other along the way. Our individual dreams matter, and I want us to make room for them while continuing to build something meaningful together.

I hope we keep meeting difficult moments with honesty instead of assumptions, gentleness instead of blame, and care instead of giving up on each other. We may not always have the perfect words, but we can keep trying to understand and support one another.

And when life gives us lighter days again, I want us to enjoy them—to make time for each other, laugh, play CODM and ML, watch together, and look forward to the moments when we can finally enjoy or meet again. I imagine us creating new memories, celebrating each other’s achievements, and sharing more of the everyday moments that make our relationship feel like ours.

I don’t know exactly what the future will bring, but I hope we continue facing it with patience, love, and the willingness to keep growing—both individually and together.
