CHAPTER XII

Twelve Months, One Story: And If I Had to Begin Again 🩵🖤🥳🤩

Twelve months may sound like a simple measure of time, but to me, sobrang special niya because within those twelve months are all the memories we made, the things we went through, and everything we shared. Andun yung bawat tampuhan, kasiyahan, games, lessons, changes, hardships, and all the little moments that slowly became part of our story. But most importantly, andun yung love natin—the love that carried us through all of it.

When I look back at our first year, I realize that our story wasn’t made from one huge moment. It was made from hundreds of little ones. The conversations, the laughter, the games, the random moments, the quiet days, the misunderstandings, the times we missed each other, the days when we were busy, and the moments when all we really needed was a little patience and understanding.

Some moments were exciting, and some were ordinary. Some made us laugh until we forgot about everything else, while others taught us how to listen, understand, and care for each other better. Some moments were easy, and some were difficult. But every single one became part of our story.

And when I think about everything we’ve been through, I don’t feel like I need to erase any of it.

Because even the difficult moments taught us something.

Even the quiet days meant something.

Even the times when we didn’t know what to say became part of the story that brought us here.

That’s why I’m grateful for this first year. Not because every day was perfect, but because it was ours.

I’m grateful for the memories we made, for the ways we grew, and for all the little things you brought into my life. I’m proud of the story we’ve written so far, not because it has been flawless, but because we kept learning and moving forward together.

And most of all, I’m grateful that I met you.

Thank you for accepting me for who I am. Thank you for staying patient with me, for understanding me even when I’m not always easy to understand, and for choosing to stand beside me through the different versions of myself. Thank you for supporting me, helping me in every way you can, encouraging me when I need it, and simply being someone I can share my life with.

I don’t think I’ll ever be able to perfectly explain how much those things mean to me.

But there is one question I think about when I look back at the beginning of everything.

If I could go back to the very beginning, knowing everything I know now, would I choose you again?

If I knew about every day we would miss each other, every time we would have to wait for the chance to meet, every quiet conversation, every misunderstanding, every difficult moment, every busy season, and every part of life that we still haven’t discovered—

would I still choose you?

My answer is yes.

I would choose you again.

I would choose to meet you again, to start those first conversations again, to slowly discover the little things that make you who you are, and to let our connection grow in its own time.

I would choose the laughter.

I would choose the games.

I would choose the random conversations.

I would choose the ordinary days.

I would choose the moments when we missed each other.

I would choose the quiet days.

I would choose the hard days.

I would choose every memory that made me smile and even the moments that taught us something difficult.

I would choose all of it again—

because all of it became us.

I would choose to keep learning how to understand you, even when it takes patience. I would choose to care for what we have through busy days and quieter seasons. I would choose to support your dreams, celebrate your growth, and keep growing beside you while we each become more fully ourselves.

And if I had to begin again, I wouldn’t ask for a different story.

I would ask for ours.

Not because I expect everything ahead to be easy, but because what we share matters enough to me that, knowing there will still be things we have to learn, moments we have to face, and days we have to get through, I would still want to write those pages with you.

So here we are.

Twelve months.

One year.

So many memories.

So many little moments that somehow became something bigger than I ever expected.

And somehow, after everything, my favorite part is still knowing that I get to call this our story.

Thank you for sharing this first year with me.

Thank you for being you.

Thank you for letting me know you.

Thank you for every laugh, every game, every conversation, every moment of patience, every little effort, and every memory you gave me.

Thank you for choosing me.

And thank you for giving me a reason to believe that this story is worth continuing.

Our first year is now a chapter we can look back on.

But it isn't where our story ends.

It's simply the first book.

And if one day we look back at this page together, I hope we smile at the two people we were when we first began—and feel proud of everything we became along the way.

Twelve months became one story.

One story became us.

And if I had to begin again, I would still choose you.

Happy 1st Monthsarry, my love.

Now, until forever and all eternity—
until everything ceases to exist. 🩵🖤
