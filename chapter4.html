CHAPTER IV

Paulit-ulit na Pagpili sa Atin

We know that love isn’t only about the happiest moments. It’s also about continuing to choose each other as we learn, grow, and make our way through life together.

These past months have taught me that being together means being willing to understand one another, to listen with an open heart, and to keep showing up in the ways we can. We’re still learning about each other, and I know there will always be more to discover. But I’m grateful that we get to keep learning side by side, one conversation and one day at a time.

Choosing you doesn’t mean every moment has to be perfect. It means caring about what we have and doing our best to nurture it. It means appreciating the happiness we share, being patient as we grow, and remembering that we’re on the same team.

Sa bawat araw na pinipili nating mag-usap, maglaan ng oras para sa isa’t isa, at intindihin ang nararamdaman ng bawat isa, mas lalo nating pinapatibay ang story natin. Those little choices may seem simple, but they mean so much to me. They show me that what we’re building is something we both treasure.

I want you to know that I’m grateful for the love and effort you bring into our relationship. Thank you for sharing your time, your thoughts, and your heart with me. Thank you for being someone I can laugh with, talk to, and keep making memories with.

As we turn the page on our first year, I hope we continue choosing each other—not just today, but in all the days and chapters still ahead. I’m excited for everything we’ll keep learning, sharing, and building together.

I choose you, my love, and I’m grateful that we get to keep choosing us.
