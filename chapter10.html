CHAPTER X

The Little World We Share 🩵🎮🖤

There’s a little world that belongs to us, a world made up of our conversations, laughter, shared jokes, and the games we play together. It may seem simple, but those moments have become a special part of my life.

I love that we can spend time together even when we’re apart. Playing CODM, ML, Roblox, and WePlay, and also talking between matches, teasing each other, or just sharing a laugh can turn an ordinary day into one I’ll remember. It’s not only about winning or playing well; it’s about having fun with you and feeling like we’re sharing the moment.

Some of the things we do may seem ordinary, but they mean a lot to me. A message, a little update, making time to play, or asking about my day can make me feel remembered and cared for. I notice those things, and I’m grateful for them.

Gusto kong malaman mo na pinahahalagahan ko ang maliliit na sandali natin, kahit simpleng usapan lang, tawanan, o paglalaro. They remind me that our connection is built not only from big occasions, but also from the little ways we show up for each other and give love.

I hope we keep adding new memories to our little world: more matches, more stories, more laughter, even sad times, and more moments that feel like us. Wherever we are, I’ll always treasure the time we get to share.
