CHAPTER V

Ang Mga Susunod na Kabanata 🍀

As we celebrate our first year together, I can’t help but feel excited for everything that’s still waiting for us. We’ve already shared so many precious moments, and I know our story still has a lot of pages left to write.

Hindi man natin alam kung ano ang naghihintay sa atin sa mga susunod na araw, masaya akong harapin ’yon kasama ka. We’ll keep learning about each other, making memories, and finding new reasons to appreciate what we have. I look forward to the simple days just as much as the special ones, because having you by my side makes them feel meaningful and really special.

I hope we keep supporting each other’s dreams and celebrating every little step forward. I hope we continue to make each other laugh, listen when one of us needs to be heard, and hold on to the closeness we’ve built. Whatever the next chapter brings, I want us to meet it with patience, care, and love.

Thank you for being part of my life and for making this first year something I’ll always treasure. I’m proud of the memories we’ve made and grateful for the connection we continue to build. Every day with you gives me another reason to look forward to what comes next.

This is only the beginning of all the chapters we still get to share. And as we turn the page, there’s one thing I know for sure:

I’m looking forward to the future because I get to imagine it with you, my lalanz. 🩵
