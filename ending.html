THE END OF BOOK I

The Last Page,
and the First Yes

✦

If I could return to the beginning—not knowing how long the distance would feel, how much I would miss you, or how many things we would still have to learn—I would still choose the moment our story began.

Not because I knew it would always be easy.

I didn’t.

But because alam kong worth it tayo.

I couldn’t have known every quiet ache, every wait, every misunderstanding, or every day when all I wanted was to be beside you. I couldn’t have known how much twelve months could change me, or how someone I once simply met could become someone I would be so grateful to have found in this enormous world.

But I would still choose it.

I would choose your heart, your soul, your voice, our little conversations, the games we play, the laughter, the ordinary days, and even the hard days that somehow made us stronger and closer.

I would choose the memories we’ve made.

And I would choose the memories we haven’t made yet.

I would choose the chance to keep learning you, cheering for you, supporting you, and watching us grow into whatever comes next.

A year ago, I couldn’t have known what these twelve months would come to mean.

Now I know.

They became a piece of my life I would never trade—not because every page was perfect, and not because every moment was extraordinary, but because every page brought me closer to you.

So if time turned back and placed me at the beginning again, with the whole story still unwritten, I wouldn’t ask to know how it ends.

I would only ask one thing:

“Is he still there?”

And if I saw you—somehow, somewhere—waiting to become part of my story...

I already know what I would say.

“I would choose you again.”

Because if I had to start all over, I wouldn't ask for a different story.

I would ask for ours.

Maybe this is where the page ends.

But not us.

Our first year is now something we can look back on—a collection of memories, laughter, games, difficult moments, growth, love, and all the little things that became ours.

I made this book because I never wanted our first year to become something we simply remembered and eventually forgot.

I wanted to give our memories a place to stay.

A place we could return to someday and say:

“Look how far we’ve come.”

So if you ever read these pages again, I hope you remember what they were made from.

They were made from us.

From the two people who met, slowly became close, learned each other, struggled sometimes, laughed together, grew together, and somehow reached this page.

Our first year is over.

But our story isn't.

There are still places we haven't been, memories we haven't made, conversations we haven't had, dreams we haven't reached, and countless pages we haven't written yet.

So I won't call this goodbye.

I'll call it:

The end of our first book.

And maybe someday, when enough time has passed, we'll look back at this little time capsule and remember who we were here.

Two people.

One beginning.

Twelve months.

One unforgettable first year.

And a story that still has more to tell.

Happy first anniversary, my love.

Thank you for being my favorite part of this chapter.

Book I: Complete.

And if the story ever asks me the same question again—

“Would you choose him?”

My answer will always be:

“Yes. You. Still you.”
